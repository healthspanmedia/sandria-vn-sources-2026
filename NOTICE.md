# Notice

## What these files record

This is a record of which websites four AI assistants cited in answer to a fixed
set of questions, on a stated date. It records the citations, not the answers.

## Nothing here is verified as true

No cited website was checked for accuracy. A website appearing in these files
means an assistant attached it to a statement in an answer. It does not mean the
website is correct, current or reliable, or that the statement it was attached
to is true.

## No statement about any website or business is made or endorsed

The publisher makes no statement about any website or business in these files.
The type and scope codes describe what a website is for and where the brand that
runs it is based. They say nothing about its quality, standing or
trustworthiness.

## Nothing here rates, ranks or assesses any business

These files do not rate, rank, score or assess any business, and they are not a
directory, a recommendation or a guide to choosing a provider. How often an
assistant cited a website is a measurement of the assistant's behavior, not of
the business or the website.

Nothing here is medical, legal or financial advice.

## Corrections

Corrections are welcome, including from the operator of a website who believes
it is classified wrongly. A correction cannot change what an assistant cited,
since that is the observation. It can be recorded alongside the observation, and
it can correct the type, the scope or the preparation.

Route corrections to hello@sandria.ai, or to the publisher, Sandria OÜ,
registry code 17585074.
